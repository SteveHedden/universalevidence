A five-minute guide

How to use Universal Evidence

Follow one Malnutrition search from a question to its studies and evidence graph. No specialist vocabulary is required.

Choose a State Read results Browse terms Use Graph

  1. Step 1

    Choose Malnutrition as the State

    State is Universal Evidence’s name for a condition or outcome—the situation you want to understand or change. Search starts with a Malaria chip. Use its Remove Malaria control, type Malnutrition, and choose the canonical Malnutrition suggestion. Selecting the suggestion, instead of leaving typed text in the field, connects your search to the State thesaurus.

  2. Step 2

    Decide how much to narrow

    Leave Intervention as ANYTHING to discover which interventions appear in Malnutrition evidence. Or choose a canonical Intervention suggestion to focus the search. Region is optional; use it only when location matters.

    Filled axes combine: Malnutrition plus an Intervention plus a Region means studies must satisfy all three. When you add multiple chips within one axis, choose Match any for alternatives or Match all when every selected term must be present.

    Good first search State: Malnutrition Intervention: ANYTHING Region: ANYWHERE

  3. Step 3

    Search, then open an Intervention group

    Press Search. With Malnutrition selected, results are grouped by Intervention. Open a group to see its contributing study cards.

    Group names are labels from the Universal Evidence Intervention thesaurus. Study titles, IDs, registry badges, locations, and study details come from the source registry. A shared thesaurus label organizes those source records; it does not replace or rewrite them.

  4. Step 4 · Explore terms

    Browse the thesaurus when you need the right term

    Click Browse beside State or Intervention. Use Filter, expand or collapse branches, and select + Add. The State and Intervention thesauri show broader and narrower relationships, helping you discover terms around Malnutrition that you may not know to type.

    Browse is available on desktop and phone screens. On a phone, close the thesaurus drawer after choosing a term to return to your search.

  5. Step 5

    Carry the canonical selection into Graph

    Once Malnutrition—or any canonical State or Intervention—is selected, choose Graph above the search workspace. Graph requires at least one State or Intervention. It will not hand off unresolved typed Region text or Outcome chips; select a canonical Region and remove Outcome chips first.

    Before opening Graph ✓ Malnutrition is a selected chip ✓ Region is selected or empty ✓ No Outcome chips remain

  6. Step 6

    Explore Malnutrition’s evidence connections

    Blue nodes are States and green nodes are Interventions. Larger nodes have more linked studies. Hover or click a node to focus its neighbors; pan, zoom, and drag nodes to explore. These lines are evidence connections, not broader or narrower thesaurus relationships, and do not mean that the connected concepts are equivalent.

    Click a State–Intervention connection to open Evidence details with the linked-study count, source breakdown, and contributing study links. Refine the selected terms and rebuild the graph whenever you want a different Malnutrition view. Use Universal Evidence search to return.
