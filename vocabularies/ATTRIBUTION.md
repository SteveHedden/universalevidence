# Sources and attribution

This distribution combines Universal Evidence's editorial contributions with source terminology and external URI alignments. The vocabulary's CC BY 4.0 license does not replace the rights or attribution of upstream sources. The versioned [States source manifest](https://github.com/SteveHedden/universalevidence-conditions-outcomes-thesaurus/blob/v0.2.0/source-manifest.json) and [Interventions source manifest](https://github.com/SteveHedden/universalevidence-interventions-thesaurus/blob/v0.4.0/source-manifest.json) separate identified verbatim source definitions, edited descriptions, retained bibliographic citations, and URI alignments. A mapping is not evidence that text was copied from its target.

## National Library of Medicine

**Courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine.** NLM does not endorse Universal Evidence. This edited snapshot does not necessarily reflect the most current or accurate NLM data.

[Medical Subject Headings (MeSH)](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/meshhome.html) supplies medical terminology, scope descriptions, and descriptor alignments. Applicable [NLM data terms](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/databases/download/terms_and_conditions.html) require acknowledgement, no implication of endorsement, and current data or a clear notice about currency. Snapshot hashes are recorded in the manifest; a complete upstream release identifier was not retained in the source snapshots, so they are not represented as a current MeSH release.

Imported passages with identifiable textbook citations have been replaced with concise editorial descriptions. Bibliographic citations remain in `dcterms:bibliographicCitation`; linked source records remain in `dcterms:source` where identified. These citations document provenance and do not represent independent verification of every statement in the cited work. [NLM copyright guidance](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/web_policies.html#copyright) distinguishes government work from third-party content; no blanket relicensing of third-party publications is asserted.

Selected edited descriptions also draw on [MedlinePlus Genetics](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/) and the [MedlinePlus Medical Encyclopedia](https://medlineplus.gov/encyclopedia.html). The relevant pages are cited in the data; their prose is summarized rather than reproduced.

## Development and statistical vocabularies

- **United Nations Statistics Division:** [SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/) supplies indicator identifiers and titles. Specific measurement scope, population, and units remain significant. An indicator's identity is distinct from a related state or intervention.
- **United Nations Dag Hammarskjöld Library:** [UN Thesaurus](https://metadata.un.org/) supplies topical URI alignments. These links do not redistribute the UN thesaurus or claim ownership of it.
- **OECD:** DAC/CRS purpose-code labels inform aid-purpose terminology and alignments. The [OECD Open Access Policy](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/oecd-open-by-default-policy.html) distinguishes content published before and after 1 July 2024; linked OECD material remains subject to its applicable terms. The local code-list snapshot did not retain a dated publication identifier. No OECD logo is reproduced or endorsement implied.
- **World Health Organization:** International Classification of Diseases, Eleventh Revision (ICD-11), WHO, [online classification](https://icd.who.int/browse11). ICD identifiers are external references, not a redistribution or modification of the WHO classification. See the [WHO classification licensing information](https://www.who.int/standards/classifications/classification-of-diseases).
- **World Bank:** [World Development Indicators](https://databank.worldbank.org/source/world-development-indicators) supplies series identifiers, names, and unit conventions. A historical base year in a series name is retained as part of that series' meaning and is not a claim that the series is current.

No AGROVOC URI alignments or verbatim definition matches were found in these release files when compared with the available AGROVOC snapshot. This finding avoids a blanket license claim: FAO's [AGROVOC access page](https://aims.fao.org/standards/agrovoc/access-agrovoc) describes CC BY IGO 3.0 for its six official-language datasets, but those terms do not independently establish the origin of an untraced paraphrase.

## Limits of the inventory

Exact-text comparison identifies copying that can be evidenced from the available snapshots. It does not recover missing historical provenance or certify every concept's scientific correctness. Unmatched descriptions are explicitly marked as such in the manifest. Third-party texts, logos, and complete upstream datasets are not offered under a new license by this distribution.
