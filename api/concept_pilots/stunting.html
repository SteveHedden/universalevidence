Conditions & outcomes

Stunting

The condition of impaired linear growth in children, defined as height-for-age more than two standard deviations below the WHO Child Growth Standards median, reflecting chronic undernutrition.

Selected evidence

ClinicalTrials.gov · Zimbabwe · Cluster-randomized trial

Supplements + feeding counselling

21% lower stunting prevalence at 18 months in the SHINE trial—a relative reduction (RR 0.79; 95% CI 0.72–0.87).

Study record ↗ · Humphrey et al., 2019 ↗
ISRCTN · Togo · Cluster-randomized trial

Cash transfers alongside community services

Adding cash transfers to shared community services did not produce a statistically conclusive reduction in stunting (relative odds ratio 0.74; 95% CI 0.51–1.06).

Study record ↗ · Briaux et al., 2020 ↗
AEA · Indonesia · Cluster-randomized trial

Community nutrition and sanitation programme

No statistically conclusive effect on stunting among children aged 0–35 months: +0.3 percentage points versus control (95% CI −2.3 to +2.9).

Study record ↗ · Beatty et al., 2024 ↗

WHO ICTRP: no finding selected yet.
